Privacy policy

Last updated 11 August 2026 · Applies to the Nomkin mobile app on iOS and Android

Nomkin keeps everything on your phone. There is no account, no server of ours, and nothing that follows you around. Nothing you log is ever sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

Who this is from

Nomkin is made and published by an independent developer in the United Kingdom. For anything in this policy, write to hello@nomkin.app.

What Nomkin collects, how, and what it is used for

Everything in this table is created on your device and stored on your device, in the app's own private storage. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

What How it is collected What it is used for
Your profile — pet name and species, date of birth, sex, height, weight, activity level and goal You enter it during setup, and can change it in Settings Working out your daily calorie and step targets
Food you log — items, portions and calories, with a timestamp You add it by scanning, searching, or typing an estimate Filling the Food bar and showing your day and your history
Weight check-ins, if you choose to do them You enter them Adjusting your target over time and drawing your trend
Step count — the number of steps you have taken today Read from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android), only if you say yes Filling the Move bar
Coins and collected items Earned in the app, or bought through the App Store or Google Play The shop and the things your pet wears
Settings — reminders, units, reduced motion and the like You choose them Making the app behave the way you asked
A cache of food data you have looked up Saved automatically after a barcode scan or a search So the same item logs instantly next time, with no network

Nomkin does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, advertising identifier, or any other device identifier. It has no sign-in and no user account of any kind.

Health and step data

Nomkin asks your phone's health store — Apple Health on iPhone, Health Connect on Android — for one thing: the number of steps taken during the day currently on screen.

  • It reads step counts only. It requests no other category of health or fitness data.
  • It only ever reads. Nomkin never writes anything back to your health store.
  • The step count stays on your device and is used solely to fill the Move bar. It is never used for advertising, marketing, or any use-based data mining, and it is never sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party — including any service provider or data broker.
  • Connecting is entirely optional. Decline, and Nomkin works exactly as before; you top up the Move bar yourself by tapping it.

You can withdraw this permission at any time — in Apple Health under Sources, or in the Health Connect app on Android — and Nomkin will stop reading immediately. On Android, Health Connect also revokes the permission by itself if Nomkin has not read anything for about thirty days. Nothing you have already logged is lost when access ends; the Move bar simply goes quiet until you say yes again.

The camera

If you scan a barcode, Nomkin uses your camera to read the barcode number and nothing else. The barcode is decoded on your device by the operating system's own scanner. No photograph or video is taken, stored, or transmitted, and you can always type the numbers instead — or skip scanning entirely and search or quick-add.

The one thing that leaves your phone

When you scan a barcode or search for a food, Nomkin asks Open Food Facts — a free, open food database run by a French non-profit — what that product is.

  • What travels is the barcode number, or the words you typed, and nothing else. No profile, no calories, no weight, no steps, and no identifier for you or your phone.
  • Open Food Facts will see the network request and therefore your IP address, as any website does. It is not told who you are, and it is not sent anything else.
  • What comes back is cached on your device, so scanning the same item again works with no network at all.
  • This is the only network request Nomkin makes for its own features.

Open Food Facts has its own privacy policy. We do not share any user data with them beyond the search term or barcode described above, and we do not receive any user data from them.

If you buy coins

Optional coin packs are sold through Apple's App Store on iPhone and Google Play on Android. The payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google. Nomkin never sees and never receives your card details, billing address, or store account. It is told which pack was purchased so it can add the coins, and that is the whole of it. Those purchases are governed by Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Third parties, and the protection they give your data

Nomkin shares user data with no one. There are no analytics services, no advertising networks, no crash reporting, no attribution or marketing SDKs, and no third-party tracking libraries inside the app. Nothing counts how often you open it or what you tap. Nomkin does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework because it has nothing to ask for.

The only outside parties involved at all are the two described above — Open Food Facts, which receives a barcode or a search term and no user data, and Apple or Google, which process a purchase if you make one. There are no parent, subsidiary, or related entities with access to user data, because there is no user data held anywhere but your own phone. To the extent any of these parties receives anything in connection with Nomkin, they are required to provide the same or equal protection of user data as is stated in this policy and as required by the App Store Review Guidelines.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, Nomkin schedules them on your device using the operating system's local notification service. They are composed and delivered on the phone itself. No push server is involved, and no notification token or message content is sent anywhere.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Your data is kept on your device for as long as you keep the app, and we have no ability to access, retain, or recover any of it. There is no backup on our side, because there is no side.

  • Delete an entry: tap any logged item and delete it. Nothing is irreversible in the way that matters, and yesterday can be edited too.
  • Export everything: Settings has an export that writes everything Nomkin holds into one file you can keep or move elsewhere.
  • Delete everything: next to it is a delete that permanently empties all Nomkin data from the phone.
  • Withdraw a permission: steps, camera and notification permissions can all be withdrawn in your phone's settings at any time, and Nomkin keeps working without them.
  • Uninstall: removing the app removes everything it holds along with it.

Because none of your data reaches us, requests to us for access, correction, or erasure are not something we can act on — the controls above are the whole of it, and they are in your hands. If you have any doubt about that, please write and ask.

Your rights

Data protection law, including the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR, gives you rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability over personal data held about you. Nomkin is built so that those rights are exercised directly, by you, on your own device: everything it holds is visible in the app, editable in the app, exportable in one tap and deletable in another. We hold no copy to act on.

Children

Nomkin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal data from them — or from anyone else. Since nothing leaves the device, there is nothing for us to hold or to delete.

Changes to this policy

If Nomkin ever changes what it does with your data, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it. A change that materially affects your privacy will also be surfaced in the app rather than quietly published here.
