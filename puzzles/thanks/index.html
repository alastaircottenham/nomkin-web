Your drawing is in, and a person will look at it. If it is going into the app we will email you, and you will see it in a future update with your name underneath it.

What happens now

Every drawing is looked at by hand, which takes as long as it takes. We cannot use all of them, and we are not able to give feedback on individual submissions, so please don't read anything into a quiet inbox. It is not a comment on your work.

If you change your mind

You can withdraw a drawing at any time, for any reason, without explaining it. Write to hello@nomkin.app and it comes out of the next update. The submission terms set out exactly what that means.

Got another one in you? The submission page is still there. There is no limit on how many you send, and no advantage to sending them all at once.
