Send us a drawing

What to send

  • 2400 × 1600 px

    Landscape, that exact size. It drops straight into the frame with nothing cropped off.

  • PNG, under 10 MB

    One flat file. Not a folder of layers, and not the project file it came out of.

  • Edge to edge

    The drawing fills the whole rectangle. No border, no white frame, nothing vital against the outside edge.

  • Flat colour

    The app's art is flat and warm. Heavy photographic texture goes muddy at phone size.

Before you draw

Hand-drawn by a person

No AI-generated pictures, no tracing somebody else's work, and no photographs. We pride ourselves on using real artwork in Nomkin, giving it character AI can never create.

18 and over

Sending a drawing means agreeing to the submission terms, so we need you to be an adult.
