Send us a drawing

For artists 18 and over · Drawn by hand · You keep the copyright

Every day, Nomkin shows one picture cut into five pieces, and the pieces turn over as the day goes along. Those pictures have to come from somewhere. If you draw, we would love it to be you. You keep your drawing and your copyright, your name goes under it in the app, and you can ask for it back out whenever you like.

Drawn by hand, by a person

We don't accept AI-generated pictures. Not as a technicality, and not because of where the training data came from. The pictures in Nomkin are drawn by people, and somebody opening the app on a Tuesday is looking at a thing another person made. A generated image cannot do that job, however good it looks.

The same goes for tracing somebody else's work, and for photographs. Pencil, ink, paint, crayon, a drawing tablet, vectors, pixels: all of that is drawing, and all of it is welcome. Prompting is not.

Who can send one

You need to be 18 or over. Sending us a drawing means agreeing to the submission terms.

If you are under 18, please keep drawing. We would genuinely love to see your work when you are old enough to send it in.

You do not need to be a professional, or to have drawn anything for anybody before. Plenty of the best pictures for something like this come from people who draw for themselves.

The size and the file

  • 2400 by 1600 pixels, landscape.
  • PNG, under 10 MB. One file. Please don't send a folder of layers or a project file.
  • Edge to edge. The drawing fills the whole rectangle. No border, no white frame, no transparent margin around it, and nothing important right against the outside edge.
  • Flat colour works best. The app's own art is flat and vector-ish with warm colour, and that is the company your picture will be keeping. Heavy photographic texture tends to go muddy once it is scaled down to phone size.

Your drawing stays yours

You keep the copyright. We are asking to borrow it, not to own it, and the permission you give is non‑exclusive: you can sell it, print it, post it or license it to somebody else, and you never need to ask us. If you want it out of the app, write to us and it comes out of the next update, no reason needed.

Submissions are unpaid. Nomkin is free, it sells an in‑app currency and it can show rewarded videos to people who have switched them on, so it can make money on a day your drawing is showing, and you get no share of that.

The full detail, including what the app does to the file and what we cannot undo once somebody has installed an old version, is in the submission terms. They are short, and worth the two minutes.

What happens after you send it

We review every submission. We cannot use them all, and a drawing that does not go in is usually about fit rather than quality: the style, or the way it sits in five pieces, or simply that we already have plenty in hand.

We are not able to give feedback on individual submissions, and we cannot promise a reply either way, so please don't read anything into a quiet inbox. If yours is going in, we will email you.

The pictures are built into the app itself, so even a yes means a future update rather than tomorrow. There is no limit on how many you send, and no advantage in sending them all at once.
