Delete your account and data

Last updated 20 August 2026 · For the Nomkin mobile app on iOS and Android

Nomkin keeps your data on your phone. The only copy anywhere else is the one held for you if you chose to make a free backup account — and deleting that account deletes the copy with it, straight away and for good.

One other thing leaves the phone, and it is not a copy of anything of yours: an anonymous note once a day about how the app itself was used, which carries counts of taps and screens and no identifier of any kind. There is nothing in it to delete on request, because nothing in it says whose it is. What you can do, at any time, is stop it: Settings → Your data → Your privacy → Share how you use it. Turning it off also throws away anything counted but not yet sent.

Delete your account and the backup copy

In the Nomkin app:

  1. Open Settings (the cog on the home screen).
  2. Under Your data, tap Cloud backup.
  3. Tap Delete the account, then confirm with Yes, delete the account. You will be asked for your password, because the account provider requires a recent sign-in before a deletion.

What is deleted: the backup copy of your data, and the account itself. The stored copy is removed first, then the account. Both are permanent, and neither can be restored by us or by you.

What is deleted from the copy: your profile, every day you logged and the step total on it, your food entries, your water, your weight check-ins, your coins, collection and purchase records, and your settings.

What is kept: nothing. There is no deactivated state, no grace period and no archive of earlier copies — the account holds one copy, replaced each time, and it goes when the account does. Residual copies may persist briefly in Google's own routine system backups before being overwritten in the ordinary course. Your email address goes with the account.

What is untouched: everything Nomkin holds on your phone. Deleting the account does not remove a single entry from the app in front of you.

Delete everything on your phone

This is a separate control, and it is the one that empties the app itself:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Under Your data, tap Start over, or delete.
  3. Tap Delete everything, then Yes, delete it all.

This permanently empties your profile, every day logged, your nomkin, your coins and your collection from the phone. It does not touch the backup copy, so a delete on the phone still leaves you a way back. Uninstalling the app removes everything on the phone too — but it does not delete a backup account, so delete the account first if that is what you want.

If you cannot sign in

If you have forgotten your password, use Forgotten your password? on the sign-in screen and we will email you a link. If you cannot get into the account at all, write to hello@nomkin.app from the address the account was made with, and we will delete it for you.
