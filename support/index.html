From inside the app

If you have the app in front of you, Settings → Send feedback is the shorter way round. It reaches the same address, and it carries the app’s version number and which kind of phone it is on, so we do not have to ask. Nothing from your day goes with it. What travels is listed on the privacy page.

What helps us help you

If something went wrong, three things make it much quicker to sort out:

  • What you were doing, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • Which phone it is, and whether the app came from the App Store or Google Play.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.

None of it is required. Write in whatever form suits you and we will ask if we need more.

Your account and your data

If you have forgotten your password, use Forgotten your password? on the screen where you sign in, and we will email you a link. The steps for deleting the backup account, or everything the app holds on your phone, are on the account and data deletion page. If you cannot get into the account at all, write to us from the address it was made with and we will delete it for you.

What Nomkin does with your data is in the privacy policy, and the rules the app is used under are in the terms of use.
