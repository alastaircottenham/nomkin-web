Nomkin is a general wellbeing and habit app. Calorie targets, water targets, step targets and weight trends in it are estimates, calculated from figures you typed in using ordinary published formulas. They are not a diagnosis, a treatment, a diet plan or clinical advice, and Nomkin is not a medical device.

Do not rely on it for medical decisions. Talk to a doctor or a qualified health professional before changing what you eat or how you exercise, particularly if you are pregnant, under 18, managing a health condition, or have a history of disordered eating. If food or weight tracking is not good for you, please stop using the app — that is a perfectly good outcome, and the delete button is right there in Settings.

Food data comes largely from Open Food Facts, a community-maintained open database. It is very good and it is not perfect — entries can be incomplete or wrong. Check the packet if it matters.
